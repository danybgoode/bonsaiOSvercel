## Core Principles

1. **Validate before building.** Every feature is a hypothesis. Test it cheaply before investing.
2. **The North Star is the filter.** If it doesn’t move an input metric, it doesn’t get built.
3. **Financial visibility is not optional.** Product teams that can’t read their P&L are cost centers. Teams that can are revenue engines.
4. **Leverage existing tools.** We don’t add tools. We orchestrate what you have.
5. **Build is not done until you’ve measured.** Shipping is the beginning, not the end.

## The Three Games (North Star Framework)

Every product plays one of three games:

- **Attention** — time in product (media, social, entertainment)
- **Transaction** — number of transactions driven (commerce, fintech, marketplace)
- **Productivity** — efficiency getting a job done (SaaS tools, dev tools, B2B)

Pick one. The North Star metric follows from the game.

---

## The Four Inputs

Every North Star metric is driven by four inputs:

- **Breadth** — how many customers are doing the core action?
- **Depth** — how completely are they doing it?
- **Frequency** — how often are they doing it?
- **Efficiency** — how fast do they get to value?

Improving any input should move the NSM. If it doesn’t, the input is wrong.

---

## The Greenfield Test

After defining your North Star and inputs, spend two minutes listing every opportunity you can think of to move each input. If you can’t generate at least 5 opportunities per input, the input is probably wrong or too vague.

## The Highest Domino

In any strategy, one assumption is more dangerous than the others. If it’s wrong, everything else falls. The Deliberate Risk Validation workshop identifies this assumption and designs the cheapest possible test to validate it before you build.
